## Scope

Use this mode pack when the user asks for practical improvements to an existing
system, process, design, or workflow.

## MCM Routing Guidance

Use deterministic MCM when improvements can be compared by bounded numeric
criteria such as savings, capacity, margin, cost, risk, or implementation
burden.

## Reporting Guidance

Prioritize feasible improvements, identify tradeoffs, and keep speculative
ideas clearly separated from computed findings.
